Questions, answered

Is the free version really free?

Yes. You can generate as many quick matches as you like. We don’t store your data until Supabase consent flows ship.

What measurements do I need?

At minimum: birthday (for age), height, and weight. Optional measurements—arm span, leg inseam, shoulder, hip, hand, foot-length—boost accuracy.

When do paid features arrive?

We’re finalizing Stripe Checkout now. Paid adult analysis and child forecasts launch soon after, with email waitlist rolling out first.

How do child forecasts work?

Two guardians consent, share the child’s measurements, and optionally include parent data. Sporty projects the child’s future body metrics to recommend sports that support long-term development.
